# Landing page checks

Checks specific to landing pages, on top of the general design files. They are about the page's design doing its job: making the offer and the next step obvious. They are not copy critique — wording problems go to a copy pass.

## First screen
- **What it is and who it's for is readable in the first screen** at 1280×800 and at 390×844. A headline that only says how it makes you feel ("Work, reimagined") with no product noun forces the visitor to scroll to understand. *Reason: most visitors decide to stay or leave from the first screen.*
- **One primary CTA is visible without scrolling** on desktop and mobile, and it's the most prominent interactive element there. *Reason: the page has one job; the visitor shouldn't hunt for it.*
- **A product visual (UI, output, or demo) appears in or right after the first screen.** *Reason: showing beats describing; abstract illustrations don't prove the product exists.*
- **The headline is the largest text on the page** and nothing in the hero competes with it (no equally loud badge, stat or logo). *Reason: two focal points means none.*

## CTAs
- **One primary button style, used for the same action everywhere.** Secondary actions (watch demo, contact sales) are visibly quieter: outline, ghost, or text link. *Reason: identical buttons for different actions make the visitor choose instead of act.*
- **CTA labels name the action** ("Start free trial", "Get the template"), not "Submit"/"Click here". (Design check only: flag it, leave the rewording to a copy pass.)
- **Repeated CTAs lower on the page** (after pricing, at the end) use the same primary style. *Reason: scanners who reach the bottom need the same door.*

## Proof
- **Proof sits near the decision points:** logos or a rating near the hero CTA, testimonials near pricing. *Reason: trust is needed at the moment of commitment, not in an isolated block.*
- **Testimonials carry a name, role or company, and ideally a photo.** Anonymous quotes read as invented.
- **Logo rows are muted (greyscale or one tone) and consistently sized** so no single logo shouts.

## Section rhythm
- **Sections are visibly separated** by background change, generous spacing, or a divider — not a uniform stack of white blocks with equal gaps. *Reason: the eye needs to know where one idea ends.*
- **Vertical spacing between sections is clearly larger than spacing within them.** *Reason: grouping by proximity (see spacing.md).*
- **Each section has one heading level that's consistent across the page** (all section titles the same size/weight).
- **Text columns stay readable**: 45–75 characters per line for body copy, even in wide sections.

## Mobile (390px)
- **No horizontal scroll** and nothing clipped at the right edge.
- **Headline doesn't break into one or two words per line**; type scales down.
- **Rows of 3+ peer cards don't turn into a very long single-column stack**; consider a swipe row or a 2-column grid.
- **Tap targets ≥ 44px tall**, and the primary CTA is full-width or close to it.
- **Desktop-only padding is reduced**; sections aren't separated by a screen of empty space.
