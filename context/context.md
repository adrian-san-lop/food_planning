## Data and safety rules

- Keep public generic catalogs separate from personal profiles and activity data. Raw recipe ideas live in the private Supabase proposal inbox until manual AI-assisted catalog work is published.
- State format v5 is the oldest local browser state migrated; the current state format is v12. Keep v5-v11 local-state migrations plus v12 and do not support v1-v4 or personal JSON restore. Migration converts old recipe usage snapshots, removes legacy shortfall properties, and preserves stock movements in their historical inventory units.
- `food.unit` is the pantry/purchase unit and `food.recipeUnit` is the recipe unit. Whole fruit portions use `ud` in recipes and pantry/purchases; chopped vegetables and partial produce portions use recipe `g` and may use pantry/purchase `ud`. Bread de molde uses whole slices (`ud`), up to two per meal, in recipes and grams in pantry/purchases, converted at approximately 25 g per slice. Eggs use `ud` and liquids use `ml`; milk is purchased in 1 L cartons.
- A completed meal keeps its full recipe usage, deducts only available stock, and does not calculate or store a shortfall. Shopping covers pending meals only. Pantry balances stay at or above zero; reversals return only the amount deducted.
- Legacy strength-day flags may remain in existing local state but are inert; fixed-menu third-amount overrides were removed. Activity sessions, weights, pantry movements, menu cycles, and shopping selections sync to their corresponding tables.
- ADR-0008 accepts immutable per-ingredient completion snapshots in recipe units, separate from inventory movements, with indefinite retention and no scheduled purge. Track database size as users are added; review near 350 MB. ADR-0009 accepts email + password, open registration, and no email confirmation. ADR-0010 records relational sync and its one-time import. Package 7 verifies personal-state isolation with two real Auth accounts; the owner confirms the current version works on PC and mobile.
- No offline queue. The UI must only report a successful remote save after Supabase confirms it; on failure it keeps the local working copy and shows pending/error status. Ordinary cross-device changes reconcile automatically; confirmed full reset remains a separate operation.
